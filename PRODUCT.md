# Product

## Platform

web

## Stack

Delegated: static semantic HTML + modular CSS (tokens/base/components/sections) + vanilla ES-module JS with a
data layer (`js/data.js`) driving navigation, search, product/solution/project collections and the solution
finder — chosen because the brief demands a CMS-ready, data-driven, component-like architecture that runs on a
plain local server with no build tooling. Production deployment would move the data layer behind SSR/CDN.

## Users

- **Homeowners & villa owners** — researching a lift for a private residence; job: understand options, feel safe
  about quality, reach a consultation.
- **Architects, interior designers, builders, developers** — specifying vertical mobility for projects; job:
  find credible engineering, configurations, technical resources, and a project team contact.
- **Institutional clients** (hospitals, hotels, offices, retail, industrial) — job: match the right lift to
  building usage, traffic and load, then plan installation.
- **Existing lift owners** — job: request maintenance/AMC, repair, or a modernization assessment.
- Market: India, with home base Indore, Madhya Pradesh.

## Product Purpose

Winsume Lift India Private Limited designs, engineers, installs and maintains bespoke elevators — home lifts,
passenger/commercial lifts, hospital lifts, goods lifts, glass/capsule lifts, MRL and hydraulic systems —
positioned as "engineering vertical experiences around buildings and people," not a commodity lift dealer.
Success on the homepage: every visitor type reaches a journey (explore, plan, consult, service) quickly.

## Positioning

A specialist vertical-mobility company combining custom cabin design (a luxury-interiors experience) with
serious engineering and lifecycle service — personal attention of a specialist firm with the presentation
quality of a major international elevator brand.

## Operating Context

Elevator purchase is high-consideration, safety-critical, architect-influenced, and spans new installation,
replacement, AMC/maintenance and modernization. Site must support consultation requests with floor counts,
capacity, building type; uploads of drawings; WhatsApp and phone contact (dominant channels in India).

## Product Principles

1. Content integrity over marketing: unverifiable numbers never ship unmarked.
2. Products answer "what lift?"; building solutions answer "what is right for my project?" — never merged.
3. Every audience reaches its own journey; no single generic contact funnel.
4. Less UI, more experience: typography, photography, architectural grids and whitespace carry the premium feel.
5. Motion reinforces verticality — smooth, slow, precise; accessibility never compromised.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA: keyboard navigation, visible focus, semantic HTML, accessible mega menus and search,
reduced-motion support, touch-friendly controls, mobile sticky conversion bar that never obscures content.
